Preparation Tips

Practical, actionable advice distilled from hundreds of hours of focused preparation and test-taking.

⚠️

Mistakes Beginners Should Avoid

Save months of wasted effort by steering clear of these common pitfalls.

❌ Studying Without a Plan Random reading leads to unorganized prep and burnout.
📚 Collecting Too Many Resources Resource hoarding creates confusion. Stick to standard books.
⏳ Neglecting Revision Reading once means forgetting 80%. Revise multiple times.
📝 Ignoring Mock Analysis Giving tests without analyzing mistakes yields zero improvement.
🪞 Comparing With Others Every aspirant has their own timeline. Focus strictly on your score.
